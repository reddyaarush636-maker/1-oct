The Spirit of Navratri

Navratri is a celebration of devotion, strength and the divine feminine energy. During these sacred days, devotees worship Maa Durga and celebrate with prayers, music, dance and traditional festivities.


Garba and Dandiya bring people together with colorful traditional clothing, beautiful music and joyful celebrations.
